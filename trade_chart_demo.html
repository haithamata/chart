Try changing the URL, e.g.: ?s=ETHUSDT&d=short&e=3500&sl=3600&tp=3300&i=15m&h=16 (short names: s=symbol, d=side, e=entry, i=interval, h=timeout_hours, et=entry_time, x=exit — the original long names still work too). If no entry/sl/tp are given, demo values are computed from the last close. h controls how far the shaded zone/chart extends to the right (default 8h). For a TRADE CLOSED alert, pass et (unix seconds, e.g. from the paper trade log's Time column) so the zone anchors at the actual entry bar instead of "now", and x to mark the close price.
